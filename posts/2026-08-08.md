## claudeai · Anthropic Blog

**原文網址：** [https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards)

**原文：**
> We're making updates to Claude Fable 5's biology safeguards in a way that substantially reduces false positives. Fable 5 users will now experience many fewer "fallbacks"—where the system switches to a less capable model after they make a biology-related query. In our testing, this update reduced biology-related fallbacks by about 85% across our product surfaces. Fable 5 will thus be able to assist with a wider range of biology tasks.
>
> In practice, users should see far fewer fallbacks on everyday health and educational questions—for example, interpreting lab results, understanding symptoms, and learning about biology in an educational context. Healthcare professionals will be able to receive more support from Fable 5 on clinical tasks.
>
> We believe the greatest opportunity for AI to positively affect the world is in biology and medicine, and we're investing significantly in building a responsible way to give biologists frontier access. Today, Fable still falls back to Opus 5 for requests we consider dual-use—including virology, toxicology, and molecular design—so it isn't yet usable for professional biology research and drug development. We're committed to closing that gap through trusted access pathways for frontier biology capabilities.
>
> **Why we built strong biology safeguards**
>
> Our objective is to get Fable 5's frontier capabilities into the hands of as many of our users as possible, as quickly as possible. However, to do so, we need to manage the increasing risks that come with models this capable. One such risk is in the field of biology: Fable 5 can now outperform experts on some highly complex biological tasks and provide operational support on others. That means that it can provide genuine assistance to a researcher developing a new medical treatment. But in the wrong hands, those same capabilities could be used by a malicious actor, for example in developing a biological weapon. Our capability assessments show that Fable 5 could provide significant uplift to such an actor—that is, it could provide them with capabilities they could not find anywhere else.
>
> It's often difficult to tell apart beneficial and harmful uses of AI in biology. For example, in some cases researching a treatment for a disease requires scientists to produce the dangerous compounds that cause that disease in the first place. This is most obvious for live vaccines, which require scientists to grow the same pathogen they're aiming to prevent. It's also the case for some medicines. To develop the drug captopril, which treats hypertension, scientists isolated toxic components of snake venom that crash blood pressure in humans.
>
> Sophisticated actors who wish to use our models to do harm know how to exploit this ambiguity to obscure their intent, making dangerous tasks look like ordinary research pursuits. The US Intelligence Community's 2026 Annual Threat Assessment makes clear that such actors exist, and that advances in biotechnology "could lead to novel biological threats." It notes that several state actors likely maintain active offensive biological and chemical weapons programs.
>
> Because of our concerns about these "dual-use" capabilities, we intentionally launched Fable 5 with almost all biology queries blocked. We knew this would result in a high number of false positives in the near term. Nevertheless, we chose to make this tradeoff because the cost of Fable being misused in a dual-use domain like biology could potentially be catastrophic.
>
> **How our biology safeguards work**
>
> One of the core ways we protect against misuse in biology is via safety classifiers: smaller, automated AI systems that detect when Fable 5 is asked to perform a safeguarded biology task, or produce a harmful output. When a classifier fires, the model re-routes the user's request to Opus 5, a capable model that does not have the same level of biological capability as Fable 5.
>
> Developing precise, robust classifiers is not a straightforward task. It takes time and iteration to tune the classifiers, avoiding both false positives (where classifiers fire on out-of-scope content) and false negatives (where in-scope content is missed). We also require our classifiers to be robust to attempts to bypass them (known as jailbreaks).
>
> Over the past several weeks, we've carefully rewritten the classifier's constitution (which consists of a collection of rules to help the model discern between safeguarded and allowed content), taking care to carve out benign uses in detail. We solicited feedback on the changes from a diverse range of experts (both internal and external to Anthropic). We then developed updated training data for the classifier based on that constitution, and retrained it, and verified the new classifier would still generally trigger for harmful and dual-use research biology content but would now enable a wider range of benign and beneficial uses.
>
> **Conclusions**
>
> There's still much more to be done to refine our safeguards. There will inevitably remain false positives. As we noted above, Fable will continue to block dual-use professional biology and drug development queries because of potential dual-use risk. We are fully committed to developing a safe, scalable path for researchers to use our most capable models via trusted access pathways.
>
> Footnote: As a result, we expect the total number of fallbacks—for biology-related or any other reasons—will also be reduced: by roughly 67% on Claude.ai, 55% on Cowork, 17% on Claude Code, and 7% on the Claude Platform.

**繁中改寫：**
Anthropic 正在更新 Claude Fable 5 的生物學安全防護機制，目的是大幅減少「誤判」。Fable 5 用戶以後會更少遇到「fallback（降級）」——也就是問了跟生物相關的問題，系統就自動切換到能力較弱的模型來回答的狀況。內部測試顯示，這次更新讓生物相關的 fallback 減少了大約 85%。

實務上，使用者在解讀健檢報告、了解症狀、學習生物學這類日常健康與教育類問題上，會明顯少遇到被降級的狀況；醫療專業人員在臨床任務上也能得到 Fable 5 更多支援。

Anthropic 認為 AI 對世界最大的正面影響機會就在生物與醫學領域，也正大力投資打造一套負責任的方式，讓生物學家能安全地用上前沿能力。不過目前 Fable 對於他們認定「雙重用途（dual-use）」的請求——包括病毒學、毒理學、分子設計——仍然會降級到 Opus 5，所以還不能拿來做專業生物研究或藥物開發。他們承諾會透過「可信任存取管道（trusted access pathways）」逐步補上這個缺口。

**為什麼要建立這麼嚴格的生物安全防護：** Fable 5 在某些高度複雜的生物任務上已經能超越專家表現，這代表它能真心幫助研究新療法的科學家，但同樣的能力落到惡意人士手上，也可能被用來開發生物武器。內部的能力評估顯示，Fable 5 可能對惡意行為者提供「顯著助力（uplift）」——也就是給他們原本在別處找不到的能力。生物領域裡「有益」和「有害」用途常常界線模糊：例如研發活體疫苗必須先培養出要對抗的病原體本身，開發降血壓藥物 captopril 時，科學家也是從蛇毒裡分離出讓人血壓驟降的毒性成分。老練的惡意行為者很懂得利用這種模糊地帶，把危險任務偽裝成一般研究來規避防護。美國情報體系的《2026 年度威脅評估》也明確指出，生物科技進展「可能導致新型生物威脅」，且部分國家可能維持著攻擊性生化武器計畫。正因為這種雙重用途風險可能是災難性的，Fable 5 一開始上線時刻意把幾乎所有生物相關問題都擋掉，即使這代表短期內會有大量誤判。

**防護機制怎麼運作：** 核心作法是用「安全分類器」——一種較小型、自動化的 AI 系統，專門偵測 Fable 5 是否被要求執行受管制的生物任務或產出有害內容。一旦分類器觸發，系統就會把請求改路由到 Opus 5（一個生物能力沒那麼強的模型）。要做出精準又穩健的分類器並不容易，得花時間反覆調校，同時避免誤判（把安全內容判成危險）和漏判（把危險內容放過），還要抗得住刻意規避（jailbreak）的攻擊。過去幾週，Anthropic 團隊重新改寫了分類器的判斷準則，仔細劃分出各種良性用途的界線，並徵詢內外部專家意見，據此重新訓練分類器，驗證新版仍能抓住有害與雙重用途內容，同時放行更多良性用途。

**結論：** 誤判不可能完全消除，雙重用途的專業生物研究與藥物開發查詢仍會被擋下。附註數據顯示，這次更新讓整體 fallback（不限生物相關）也一併減少：Claude.ai 約降 67%、Cowork 約降 55%、Claude Code 約降 17%、Claude Platform 約降 7%。

**核心概念（簡單說）：**
這篇文章講的是 AI 公司在「開放能力」跟「防止濫用」之間的實際取捨過程。Fable 5 上線時為了保守起見，把生物相關的問題幾乎全部擋掉、丟給能力較弱的模型回答，這種「寧可錯殺不可放過」的策略雖然安全，但也讓一般使用者（例如只是想看懂健檢報告的人）動不動就被誤判擋下來，體驗很差。這次更新的重點是透過重新訓練分類器、更精細地劃分「良性 vs. 危險」的界線，讓系統學會更準確地放行日常良性問題，同時繼續擋住真正高風險的雙重用途內容（像是病毒學、分子設計）。這本質上是機器學習裡「精準度 vs. 召回率」取捨的真實案例：一開始為了不漏掉任何危險內容（高召回率），犧牲了很多良性內容的通過率（低精準度），現在用更好的訓練資料和更細緻的規則，讓兩者同時往好的方向移動。

**前端工程師實際應用：**
- **跟你工作的關聯：** 如果你的產品有整合 Claude 模型並且服務對象包含醫療、健康類應用，這次的分類器更新會直接影響你的使用者體驗——過去可能被誤判降級的正常問題，現在有更高機率能拿到 Fable 5 完整能力的回答。
- **具體場景：** 你在做一個讓使用者上傳健檢報告請 AI 幫忙解讀的功能，過去可能常常收到「降級版模型」的較弱回答（因為觸發了生物安全分類器），這次更新後應該會明顯減少這種情況，值得重新測試一輪你的產品流程確認體驗有沒有改善。
- **這週可以嘗試：** 如果你手上有做過涉及健康/生物類查詢的 Claude 整合，把之前記錄下來「被降級」的測試案例重新跑一次，看看這次分類器更新後判斷結果有沒有變化，順便更新你內部的測試案例文件。

- Fixed MCP OAuth servers on macOS intermittently failing with a burst of 401 errors — 修復 macOS 上 MCP OAuth server 因為 keychain 讀取逾時，偶爾會連續噴出 401 錯誤、像是完全沒登入過一樣的問題。
- Fixed auto mode counting a safety-filter refusal of its own permission check toward the consecutive-block limit — 修復 auto mode 遇到安全過濾器拒絕自己的權限檢查時，會被誤算進「連續被擋次數」的問題；動作依然會被拒絕，但現在模型會被告知「繼續往下做」而不是一直重試。
- Fixed cross-session messages staying parked without a notice or expiry in headless sessions — 修復跨 session 訊息在無頭 session 或啟動過程中，會沒有通知也沒有過期時間地一直卡在那邊的問題。
- Fixed conversation history breaking on Remote Control session resume after very large conversations were compacted — 修復超大對話被壓縮（compact）過後，透過 Remote Control 恢復 session 時對話紀錄會壞掉的問題。
- Improved Remote Control: photos attached from the Claude app are now shown to Claude directly — 改善 Remote Control：從 Claude App 附加的照片現在會直接顯示給 Claude，不用再另外從硬碟讀取。
- `[VSCode]` Fixed Focus view folding away the latest to-do list, a pending question's context, and settled answers — 修復 VSCode 擴充套件的 Focus view 會把最新的待辦清單、待回答問題的上下文和已回答的內容一起摺疊消失的問題。
- `SendMessage` can now start a conversation with Remote Control sessions on other machines by name — `SendMessage` 現在可以直接用名稱主動對其他機器上的 Remote Control session 發起對話（`ListAgents` 會列出它們的名稱），不再只能被動等對方先傳訊息過來。

**核心概念（簡單說）：**
這版持續強化「跨 session、跨機器協作」的可靠性，包括 gateway 花費上限提示、跨 session 訊息（cross-session messaging）與 Remote Control 的一連串修復，同時也修了幾個跟身份驗證（OAuth token、macOS keychain）有關的頑固錯誤。整體方向是讓多個 Claude Code session 之間互相協作、遠端操控這件事更穩定可靠。

**前端工程師實際應用：**

- **具體場景：** 團隊用一台機器上跑好幾個背景 Claude Code session 分工處理不同任務，透過 `SendMessage` 讓它們互相回報進度，這次讓 `SendMessage` 可以「主動」對其他機器的 session 發起對話（不用等對方先講話），能讓協作流程更主動、更即時。
