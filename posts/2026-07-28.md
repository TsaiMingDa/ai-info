## claudeai · Anthropic Blog

**原文網址：** [https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)

**原文：**
> Our position on open-weights models
> A post by Dario Amodei, Anthropic CEO
>
> Over the last few days there has been a lot of discussion about open-weights models, especially those from China. Reports suggest that some US officials are considering banning the use of Chinese open-weights models by US companies. In response, many tech companies have signed a letter supporting open-weights models, and some people have even accused Anthropic of wanting to ban open-weights models as a means of protecting our business. Anyone who has read my past writing should know that I don't regard such bans as a useful measure, but let me state it clearly so that there is no doubt: Anthropic has never advocated for a ban on open-weights models.
>
> Open-weights models that don't have dangerous capabilities are a public good: they don't cost anything besides the compute needed to run them, and they provide value to businesses, developers, and researchers.
>
> Protectionist bans would not address my most serious national security concerns. Specifically, I am worried about two nightmare scenarios: (1) authoritarian governments building AI models more powerful than the US's and using them for military superiority or repression, and (2) powerful models being misused for cyberattacks or biological attacks, or having serious alignment problems.
>
> To address these concerns, I support three measures: not selling powerful chips or chipmaking equipment to China and cracking down on smuggling; cracking down on industrial-scale distillation operations; and requiring mandatory safety testing for all sufficiently capable models, open and closed.
>
> To summarize, we have not and are not advocating for a ban on open-weights models as a category. We should instead focus on keeping powerful chips out of authoritarian hands, stopping industrial-scale distillation, and requiring safety testing of all sufficiently capable models, open and closed.

**繁中改寫：**
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei 親自發文回應近日外界的揣測：有報導指出美國政府正考慮禁止美國企業使用中國的開放權重模型，許多科技公司也連署反對這類禁令，甚至有人指控 Anthropic 想藉此打壓開放權重模型來保護自己的商業利益。Dario 明確表態：Anthropic 從來沒有主張要禁止開放權重模型這個類別。他認為真正該擔心的國安風險，是威權政府用更強的 AI 取得軍事優勢或用來壓迫人民，以及模型被濫用來做網路攻擊或生物攻擊。他提出的解方是三管齊下：管制晶片出口與查緝走私、打擊大規模蒸餾（用別人的模型「偷學」訓練自己模型的技術）、要求所有夠強的模型不論開放封閉都要做強制安全測試。

**核心概念（簡單說）：**
這篇文章是 Anthropic 對「開放權重模型該不該被限制」爭議的正式表態。簡單說，Dario 的立場是「不要因為模型是開放的就特別針對它，該管的是能力夠強、風險夠高的模型，不管它開不開放」。他用晶片、蒸餾、安全測試這三個槓桿取代直接禁止開放模型的做法，理由是：真正想搞破壞的壞人（例如某國情報機關）本來就不會理會「美國企業不能用」這種規定，所以禁令只會傷到正當使用者，卻攔不住真正的威脅。這篇文章值得注意的地方在於，它同時反駁了「開放模型比較安全，因為大家都能檢查」這個常見說法——Dario 認為在生物安全這類領域，攻擊方能從開放模型獲得的能力，可能遠大於防禦方能因此獲得的保護。

**前端工程師實際應用：**
- **跟你工作的關聯：** 如果你的公司評估要不要用開放權重模型（像是自架 Llama、Qwen、DeepSeek 系列）做內部工具或產品功能，這篇文章點出的「安全測試」趨勢代表未來這類模型可能會被要求先過一輪安全評估才能商用。
- **具體場景：** 假設你在做一個內部知識庫 RAG 系統，原本考慮用某個中國團隊釋出的開放權重模型省成本，這篇文章提醒你要留意：未來若政策真的往「強制安全測試」方向走，選型時可能要多評估這個模型有沒有通過相關安全認證，而不只是看 benchmark 分數。
- **這週可以嘗試：** 如果你手上有專案在用開放權重模型，花點時間查一下該模型有沒有公開的安全卡（model card）或第三方安全評測報告，養成把「安全測試紀錄」也當作選型評估項目之一的習慣。
